Fluid Balance

Bladder diary · Support

A diary of what you drink and what you pass, built for the three-to-seven day record a doctor asks for. Two taps per entry, a report at the end, and nothing about you leaving your phone.

Getting in touch

Write to denacidapp@gmail.com. Say which iPhone model and iOS version you are on, and what you expected to happen — it makes the answer faster.

Common questions

I do not know the exact volume. What do I put in?
An estimate. A missed entry is worse than an inexact one, and nothing in the app marks your data as unreliable. If you did not measure at all, use Not measured — it counts towards frequency and night events, and stays out of volume totals.

Where is my data?
On your device, and in your own private iCloud database if you turn iCloud on. There is no server and no account. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I get my numbers out?
CSV export, from Settings, over your whole history, without restriction. The PDF report is laid out the way a bladder diary is normally read.

I bought Pro and it is not showing on my other device.
Use Restore Purchases in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I delete everything?
Settings, Delete all data. One step, one confirmation. It clears the device and, if enabled, your iCloud copy.

Does the app remind me to drink?
No. There is an optional daily reminder to fill in the diary — off by default, you pick the time. Its text never names what the app is about, so it gives nothing away on a locked screen.

Can the app tell me whether my numbers are normal?
No, and it never will. It records what you tell it and shows you the totals. No goals, no norms, no warnings, no advice. Interpretation belongs to your doctor.
