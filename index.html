How it works

Tap. Tap. Done.

  1. Tap + Intake or + Output

    Two buttons at the bottom of the screen. Nothing else to choose.

  2. Tap a preset

    100, 200, 250, 330 or 500 ml. Or Custom, when you have an exact number.

  3. That's the entry

    Saved with the time. No forms, no countdowns, no confirmations.

Not sure of the volume? Tap Not measured — the time still counts. If your doctor asks how strong the urge was, turn on the urge rating and tap 0 to 4 on the same sheet. Change the time or add a note when you need to. Works from the Home Screen widget and Apple Watch too.
